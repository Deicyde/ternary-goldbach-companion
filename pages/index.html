Formal companions of Helfgott's ternary Goldbach proof

Unreviewed machine audit. AI agents produced every verdict; no mathematician has checked any of it. Not affiliated with or reviewed by H. A. Helfgott, Charlie Reams or the formalization's maintainers.

Each viewer shows the PDF with every numbered item, display and proof step boxed, and the Lean declarations of calr3/ternary-goldbach at commit 192ca57 that state it, with how closely they match.

  • The ternary Goldbach problem (arXiv:1501.05438v2, 2015)

    The book: 778 numbered items, 434 displays and 993 proof steps.

  • The ternary Goldbach conjecture is true (arXiv:1312.7748v2, 2013)

    The 2013 paper: 244 numbered items, 138 displays and 317 proof steps, each matched to its counterpart in the book where it has one.

The PDFs are not hosted here: your browser fetches each one from arxiv.org, and the viewer checks its SHA-256 before showing it. Coverage by chapter, the deviations and the errata are in docs/coverage.md.
