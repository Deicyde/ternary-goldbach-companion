# Chain summaries for proof steps

Packet: proof steps of Helfgott's book/paper, each linked to a chain of several Lean ranges, given by
declaration and a one-sentence `conclusion` per range, in order. For each record write a `summary`: one
short orientation sentence (at most about 30 words) that explains the chain's mathematical progression,
how the ranges together carry the step (for example "Poisson summation turns the sum into a sum of
Fourier transforms, which the decay hypothesis then bounds term by term."). Use only what the
conclusions and the step's LaTeX say; claim nothing about the text being wrong. Name declarations in
backticks without the `TernaryGoldbach.` prefix, or not at all. Do not open other files.

Output: a JSON list `[{"id": "...", "overlay": "...", "summary": "..."}]`, one per record, in order.
Return at most two lines.
